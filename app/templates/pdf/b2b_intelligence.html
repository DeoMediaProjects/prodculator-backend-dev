How to read this report
Two readings.Charts pair graphite for what producers declared with yellow for what the Prodculator engine computed. The distance between the two is the signal.
Privacy floors.A period needs {{ report.thresholds.minimum_overall_records | default(10) }} consented signals to report at all, and any single segment needs {{ report.thresholds.minimum_segment_records | default(5) }} to be displayed. Cross-tabulations apply the same floor per cell.
Withheld, never padded.Anything under the floor is removed and labelled as withheld. It is never estimated, rounded up, or filled in.
Counts are counts.Every figure is a real count of consented, non-internal production signals. Narrative explains the numbers; it never produces them.
Two-part structure.Platform demand signals are aggregated from the pipeline. Market context is editorially curated and renders regardless of volume.

Methodology and governance
Source.Anonymised production-planning metadata submitted to Prodculator by producers who explicitly consented to aggregate Business Intelligence use. Internal and test records are excluded at the point of reading, not filtered later.
Aggregation.Monthly is the atomic unit. Each month is counted once and stored raw, and longer periods are composed from those stored months. Privacy floors are applied when the report is produced, so a segment that is too small in one month can still surface across a quarter.
Currency.Budgets are normalised to GBP at the submission-date rate before banding.
Territory semantics."Considering" is declared by the producer at intake. "Recommended" is computed by the Prodculator engine on incentive and logistics grounds. They are never merged.
Exclusions.Representation data is stored but sealed, and is excluded from every Business Intelligence read pending separate consent and legal sign-off.
Limits.These are planning signals drawn from a pipeline, not a census of the industry. They indicate direction and relative weight, not absolute market size.
